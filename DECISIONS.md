| M-06 | **The hook also fires on repeated failure, and text is also given word by word** (2026-11-18). Found in the first run: with every action proposed indifferently, Soar always has an operator, so the starting agent is never stuck (0 stuck steps in 100 episodes), and a hook on stuck alone would never fire: the agent would never learn. So the hook (`metasoar/hook.py`) fires on **stuck** (no action within `patience` decisions) and on **failing** (a level family failing at least half its training episodes, seeds 2000 and up, apart from the judge's above 10000). It writes a report and renders the request Substrate gets: intent, how it will be judged (from the base), what may change (`agent/**`), the card it must carry, and what was seen. And Soar rules match whole symbols, so a mission like "go to the red ball" was unusable as one string: the adapter now also gives any text of more than one word as `^ATTR-words`, one `^word` (`^text`, `^index`) each: a generic encoding of a Text value, not knowledge of any game. First report (20 training episodes a level): success 0.05, 0.2, 0.1, 0.3, 0.0; all five levels failing. | decided |
